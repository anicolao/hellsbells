CARD LAYOUT REVIEW

Alternate playing cards.

Large diagonal indexes on all 56 playing cards, with an individually illustrated frame for each suit. Indexes use A, 2–10, P, N, Q, K. Pentacles are gold, Cups silver, Swords iron, and Wands embossed red leather. Bridge proportions (56 × 88 mm), full-bleed court artwork, and opposing indexes keep either end readable. Material-specific full-colour suit symbols stay readable against plain suit-coloured fields.

Current and alternate

Rank order: A is low; P (Page), N (Knight), Q (Queen), K (King) follow 10. Both ends repeat the same frame, label, index, and court artwork. Number cards show exactly their rank’s worth of main pips, excluding the corner indexes. Aces and other odd ranks have a single centre symbol.
