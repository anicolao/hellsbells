COMPONENT REVIEW · BRIDGE FAMILY

One family. Two kinds of power.

One bridge-sized family: rich gold for Pentacles, silver for Cups, iron for Swords, and embossed red leather for Wands. Cups use light silver fields and dark lettering; Swords use charcoal fields and light lettering. Plain text wells keep the metallic and embossed frames readable. The original paintings stay.

All cards now use the same 56 × 88 mm proportions in play and print. Playing-card indexes are A, 2–10, P, N, Q, K.

Proposed direction

Keep the ornament. Gold, silver, iron, and crimson engraving for the four trump suits; warm copper for imps. Bells and leaves stay around the edge, leaving the portrait and text clear.

Make information immediate. Large imp names and ability headings. Round cards pair a left-aligned title with a large full-colour trump symbol; no numeral or extra icon label. Catch-up rounds carry a filled badge.

Design for the table. Upright text, one substantial portrait, and one flat rules well. These cards stay on the table, so they do not need the playing cards’ mirrored indexes.

One size: 56 × 88 mm bridge proportions across playing cards, round cards, imps, and backs. Artwork fits uniformly without stretching; print keeps the black trim edge and online play uses transparent exterior edges.
